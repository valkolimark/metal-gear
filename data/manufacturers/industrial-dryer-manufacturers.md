# Industrial Dryer Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial dryer OEMs organized by manufacturer, with their major model families. "Dryer" covers an unusually broad range of equipment — spray dryers, fluid bed dryers, rotary drum/kiln dryers, paddle and disc dryers, belt dryers, vacuum tray dryers, filter-dryers, grain dryers, and thermal sludge dryers. Many OEMs span multiple categories but dominate in one. Brand lineage is especially messy here because GEA, Andritz, and Bühler have each absorbed dozens of heritage brands over the past 30 years.

---

## Tier 1 — Global Market Leaders

The companies that dominate industrial drying globally across multiple drying technologies: GEA, Andritz, Bühler, Hosokawa Micron, and Glatt (pharma-led).

### GEA Group (Germany) — GEA Niro / GEA Process Engineering
The undisputed global leader in spray drying. GEA Niro (Soeborg, Denmark) has designed and supplied more than 10,000 spray drying plants worldwide. GEA acquired Niro A/S in 1993, and has since absorbed Aeromatic-Fielder (fluid bed), Collette (granulation), Courtoy (tableting), PHARMAG, Buck, Hampel, and Barr-Rosin. Dominant in dairy, food, pharma, chemicals, detergents, and battery materials.

**Spray Dryers (NIRO line — flagship)**
- Conventional NIRO® SD — single-stage (food ingredients, chemicals)
- Compact Dryer (CD) — whey, dense milk powder
- Tall-Form Dryer (TFD) — infant milk formula (IMF), specialty proteins
- MSD® (Multi-Stage Dryer) — milk powders, nutritional formula, coffee
- FSD® (Fluidized Spray Dryer) — agglomerated coffee, agrochemicals, vitamins
- FILTERMAT® (FMD) — heat-sensitive, sticky products
- Integrated Filter Dryer (IFD)
- VERSATILE-SD® (VSD) — ceramics, polymers, catalysts, pharma (5–265 kg/h)
- VFSD — versatile multi-stage with integrated fluid bed
- FSD MINOR® — R&D/pilot scale

- PSD® PHARMA — pharmaceutical spray dryer (cGMP)

## Common Drying Terminology (for search/listing intent)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Direct-fired** | Combustion gases contact product directly (rotary dryer, flash) |
| **Indirect-fired** | Heat transferred through wall (steam tube, drum, paddle, disc) |
| **Co-current** | Hot air and product move same direction |
| **Counter-current** | Hot air and product move opposite directions |
| **Mixed-flow** | Grain dryer design with alternating inlet/exhaust ducts |
| **Cross-flow** | Air moves perpendicular to grain flow (classic cheap grain dryer) |
| **Counter-flow** | In-bin grain drying with upward air, downward grain |
| **Fluid bed / fluidized bed** | Product suspended on cushion of hot air |
| **Flash / pneumatic** | Product entrained in hot gas stream, dried in seconds |
| **Spray / atomization** | Liquid atomized into hot air, produces powder |
| **Wurster coating** | Bottom-spray fluid bed (pharma pellets/tablets) |
| **Top spray** | Fluid bed with top-mounted nozzles (granulation) |
| **Tangential spray** | Rotor-granulator spray angle |
| **ANFD** | Agitated Nutsche Filter-Dryer (pharma combined filter + dryer) |
| **Vacuum drying** | Reduced pressure for heat-sensitive products |
| **Plug flow** | No back-mixing in continuous dryer (paddle, conveyor) |
| **GMP / cGMP** | Current Good Manufacturing Practice (pharma compliance) |
| **ATEX** | EU explosion-proof classification |
| **NFPA 68/69** | US explosion protection standards |
| **2-bar, 3-bar, 12-bar closed** | Pressure-shock resistance ratings for fluid beds |
