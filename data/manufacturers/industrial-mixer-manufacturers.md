# Industrial Mixer Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference of industrial mixer OEMs organized first by mixer type, then by manufacturer with major model families. Mixer manufacturers tend to specialize by mixer geometry (a planetary house rarely makes ribbon blenders, and vice versa) — the type-first organization reflects how buyers actually search.

---
