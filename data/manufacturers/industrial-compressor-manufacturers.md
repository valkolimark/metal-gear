# Industrial Compressor Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial compressor OEMs organized by manufacturer, with their major model families. Compressors split into two largely separate worlds: the **industrial air & gas world** (shop air, plant air, process air — dominated by Atlas Copco, Ingersoll Rand, Kaeser, and a cluster of rotary screw specialists) and the **engineered process gas world** (refinery, pipeline, LNG, petrochemical — dominated by Siemens Energy/Dresser-Rand, MAN, Baker Hughes, Mitsubishi, Elliott, and Ariel on the reciprocating side). Brand lineage (acquisitions, mergers, rebrands) is noted because the same machine often appears in the secondary market under multiple names.

## Tier 1 — Engineered Process Gas (Refinery, Pipeline, LNG, Petrochemical)

A separate world from plant air, dominated by a handful of turbomachinery houses.

### Ariel Corporation (USA, Mt. Vernon OH)
The dominant US manufacturer of separable (skid-mounted) reciprocating compressors for natural gas. Ariel does not package — packagers (Exterran, USA Compression, Archrock, Kilgore, Stewart & Stevenson, etc.) mount Ariel frames on skids with engine/motor drive.

- JGP — small frame
- JGN / JGQ — small frame, high-pressure cylinders (gas lift, CNG)
- JGJ / JGR — medium frame
- JG / JGA — medium frame, gas gathering / fuel-gas / underbalanced drilling
- JGH / JGE — medium-large
- JGK / JGT — large-frame, high BHP & rod load

**Medium-Speed Separable (large horsepower)**
- JGC, JGD, JGF — 4,000 BHP class, process/industrial and shale gas
- KBU, KBZ — very large, up to 150,000 lbs rod load
- KBB, KBV — largest, rated up to 10,000 BHP

**Hydrogen / Specialty**
- KBH — vertical hydrogen mobility compressor
- CNG-configured JGN, JGQ for vehicle fueling stations

## Notes on Brand Lineage (Important for Used-Market Listings)

The same physical machine often carries different nameplates depending on vintage:

- **Gardner Denver** = Ingersoll Rand (post-2020 merger); Joy rotary screw, Champion, CompAir, Belliss & Morcom, Reavell, Hydrovane, Robuschi, Sutorbilt, Welch, Thomas, and Nash/Garo all sit under the Ingersoll Rand umbrella today
- **Ingersoll Rand HVAC** (Trane, American Standard, Thermo King) = Trane Technologies (split from IR Industrial in 2020); not the same company as today's Ingersoll Rand
- **Dresser-Rand** = Siemens (acquired 2015) = Siemens Energy (spun off from Siemens AG 2020); product names retained (DATUM, Worthington, Clark, Turbodyne heritage)
- **GE Oil & Gas** = Baker Hughes (2017 merger, GE divested over 2018–2023); Nuovo Pignone is the Italian engineering heart of the product line
- **Nuovo Pignone** (Florence, Italy, founded 1842) = GE Oil & Gas (post-1994) = Baker Hughes (post-2017)
- **Cooper Industries Compression** = Cameron (split 1995) = Ingersoll Rand IR Energy (part, 2014) + GE/Baker Hughes (part); Superior/Ajax went to GE, TurboAir went to IR
- **Cameron Compression** = split between Ingersoll Rand (reciprocating and Turbo-Air) and Baker Hughes (Superior/Ajax engine-compressors)
- **Sullair** = Hitachi Global Air Power (since 2017 acquisition; rebranded 2023); Sullair nameplate continues in North America
- **Quincy** = Atlas Copco (since 2009); Quincy nameplate retained
- **Chicago Pneumatic** (compressors) = Atlas Copco; distinct from the CP hand-tool brand (also AC)
- **CompAir** = Gardner Denver (2008) = Ingersoll Rand (2020)
- **Champion** = Gardner Denver (2019) = Ingersoll Rand (2020)
- **Joy** (rotary screw / Turbo-Air / WN) = Gardner Denver (1989, rotary screw) + Cooper/Cameron/IR/Baker Hughes split (Turbo-Air and other lines)
- **Doosan Portable Power** = Ingersoll Rand Portable (pre-2007) = Doosan Infracore (2007–2021) = Doosan Bobcat (current); older units still bear IR nameplates
- **Worthington / Clark / Dresser Clark / Turbodyne** = Dresser Industries → Dresser-Rand (1986 JV Dresser + IR) → Siemens Energy (post-2015)
- **Sulzer Burckhardt** = Burckhardt Compression (spun out of Sulzer 2002, independent)
- **Howden** = Chart Industries (2023); Thomassen, WRV/XRV lines retained
- **Elliott** = Ebara subsidiary (since 1999); US turbomachinery heritage (formerly Carrier → Carrier-Elliott → Elliott)
- **LMF (Leobersdorfer)** = Atlas Copco (2019)
- **Nash** = Gardner Denver (2004) = Ingersoll Rand (2020); Nash-Elmo, Nash-Siemens earlier
