# Industrial Valve Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial valve OEMs organized by manufacturer, with their major product lines. Valves are the most fragmented of all industrial equipment categories — thousands of manufacturers worldwide, split across a dozen fundamental types (gate, globe, ball, butterfly, check, control, safety relief, plug, diaphragm, pinch, needle, knife gate) and dozens of service classes (API 600 refinery, API 6D pipeline, API 6A wellhead, ASME cryogenic, sanitary 3-A, nuclear N-stamp, etc.). Two mega-portfolios — Emerson and Flowserve — control most of the engineered-service brand names, with a handful of independents (Velan, Metso Valves via Neles/Valmet, Samson, IMI Critical, MOGAS) rounding out the field. Brand lineage (acquisitions, mergers, rebrands) is noted because the same valve often appears in the secondary market under multiple names depending on vintage.
