# Industrial Pump Manufacturers — Comprehensive Reference Guide

## Why Type-First Organization

Pumps are the most heterogeneous category of industrial equipment in existence. Unlike centrifuges or gearboxes — where a handful of manufacturers span most applications — pumps split sharply along two axes: **hydraulic principle** (centrifugal vs. positive displacement, with at least a dozen PD subtypes) and **application duty** (clean water, hydrocarbons at 800 °F, abrasive slurries, shear-sensitive biologics, precise chemical dosing). An ANSI process pump and a peristaltic metering pump share the word "pump" and nothing else.

This means OEMs specialize far more narrowly than they appear. Flowserve, Sulzer, and ITT/Goulds compete head-to-head in API 610 refinery service, but none of them seriously compete with Weir's Warman in copper tailings, with Watson-Marlow in pharmaceutical dosing, or with NOV's PZ-series mud pumps on a drilling rig. Buyers who search "pump manufacturers" end up lost; buyers who search "BB3 multistage" or "AODD" or "vertical turbine" find the three companies that actually matter.

This guide is organized around that reality. It opens with the **API 610 framework** — the type code every serious centrifugal pump buyer will encounter — then walks through manufacturers by specialty, then covers the positive displacement side by subtype, with a dedicated section on brand lineage. The brand lineage section is critical: Flowserve alone absorbed roughly a dozen legacy nameplates (Byron Jackson, Durco, Worthington, IDP, Pacific, United Centrifugal, and more), and all of them still appear on the used market under their original marks. A 1978 Byron Jackson barrel pump is serviced by Flowserve today, but nobody calls it a Flowserve pump.

## The API 610 Framework — Essential Taxonomy

**API 610** (equivalent to ISO 13709) is the dominant specification for centrifugal pumps in refinery, petrochemical, and upstream oil & gas service. It's so entrenched that even pumps that never see a hydrocarbon are often sold as "API-style" or quoted against API 610 requirements. Every serious centrifugal pump in secondary markets will either be API 610 compliant, ANSI B73.1 compliant, or a specialty type outside both standards.

### Overhung (OH) — Single Impeller Cantilevered on a Shaft

- **OH1** — Foot-mounted, flexibly coupled overhung. Rare in new builds; often legacy.
- **OH2** — **Centerline-mounted, flexibly coupled overhung.** The workhorse refinery process pump. Thermal expansion is absorbed symmetrically from the shaft centerline, which is why OH2 is mandated for hot service. Flowserve HPX, Sulzer OHH, Goulds 3700, and Ruhrpumpen SCE are the dominant OH2 lines.
- **OH3** — Vertical in-line, separately coupled. Lower cost than OH2 but limited to cooler, cleaner services.

### Between-Bearings (BB) — Impeller Supported on Both Ends

- **BB1** — Axially split, single- or two-stage. Horizontal split case. Used for pipeline booster, raw water, cooling water, and low-head petroleum service.
- **BB2** — Radially split, single-stage, double-suction or single-suction between bearings. More robust casing than BB1 for higher pressures.
- **BB3** — Axially split multistage ("ring section" when horizontally split). Used for boiler feed, product pipelines, water injection. The Sulzer MSD is the archetypal BB3.
- **BB4** — Single casing, radial-split multistage (legacy; largely replaced by BB5 in new builds).
- **BB5** — **Double casing, radial-split multistage.** The classic "barrel" pump. Inner element sits inside a pressure-containing barrel that never has to be broken out of the piping for maintenance. Mandatory for high-pressure boiler feed and water injection service. Flowserve HDO and Sulzer HPT are the best-known BB5 pumps.

### Vertically Suspended (VS) — Pump Hangs Below Driver

- **VS1** — Wet-pit, diffuser (vertical turbine with open pit). The classic deep-well and municipal intake pump.
- **VS2** — Wet-pit, volute.
- **VS3** — Wet-pit, axial flow. Flood control and very high flow cooling intake.
- **VS4** — Volute, line-shaft driven, sump-style process pump.
- **VS5** — Cantilever sump pump.
- **VS6** — **Double-casing diffuser can pump.** Enclosed "can" that allows VS pumps to handle volatile, low-NPSH services (hydrocarbons, condensate, LPG). A refinery staple.
- **VS7** — Double-casing, volute.

An OH2, BB3, and VS6 walk into a refinery — they're doing completely different jobs, and no single OEM truly dominates all three. Build the taxonomy, then pick the manufacturer.

## Tier 1 — Global Broad-Line Pump Manufacturers

### ITT Goulds Pumps (now part of ITT Industrial Process; parent ITT Inc.)

Goulds has been making pumps since 1848 and is effectively the defining brand of the ANSI B73.1 process pump. The Model 3196 has been in continuous production since 1961 with over one million installations worldwide, making it the most common centrifugal process pump on earth. Nearly every US chemical and light industrial facility has a 3196 somewhere in the plant.

**ANSI Process (3196 family):**
- **3196 STi/MTi/LTi/XLT-i/X17/X21** — Small-through-extra-large frame sizes
- **3196 i-FRAME** with **i-ALERT** condition monitoring — Factory-integrated vibration and temperature sensor
- **CV3196** — Recessed impeller for stringy solids
- **NM3196** — Non-metallic (FRP) for corrosive duty
- **HT3196** — High-temperature (to 700 °F) refinery-duty ANSI
- **LF3196** — Low-flow hydraulic adaptation
- **3198** — Teflon-lined for severe corrosion

**Heavy process & API 610:**
- **3175** — Larger process pump for pulp & paper, sugar, severe service
- **3700** — API 610 OH2 centerline-mount (Goulds' refinery process line; competes with Flowserve HPX)
- **3640** — API 610 OH1
- **3316/3420** — Horizontal split case BB1 for general and HVAC service
- **3311/3355/3393** — Multistage horizontal pumps (BB3 territory)
- **3410/3415** — Split case double-suction

**Slurry:**
- **HS/5500** — Hard metal slurry
- **SRL** — Rubber-lined slurry (competes with Warman)
- **JC** — Slurry-handling process

**Vertical:**
- **VIT** (Vertical Industrial Turbine), **VIC** (Can), **VIS** (Sump), **VIP** (Process)

**Budget/OEM line:**
- **TruBlue 710/712** — Basic ANSI B73.1 at a lower price point

**Goulds legacy brands (all now served under ITT/Goulds):** Allis-Chalmers Pumps, ITT A-C Pump, Marlow, Morris Pump. "A-C" pumps and Morris deep-well units still populate municipal water systems across the US.

**Note on Xylem:** Xylem and ITT Goulds are **separate companies**. Xylem was spun off from ITT in 2011 and owns Flygt, Bell & Gossett, Goulds Water Technology (residential/commercial water — different from Goulds Pumps industrial), Lowara, and Godwin. The industrial Goulds Pumps (3196, 3700, etc.) stayed with ITT.

### Flowserve

Flowserve is the product of decades of consolidation in the pump industry and is the dominant supplier of API 610 engineered pumps in North America. It was formed from the 1997 merger of Durco International (formerly BW/IP — which itself had rolled up Byron Jackson, Pacific, and United Centrifugal) with Durco. It has since absorbed further legacy lines.

**Pipeline and between-bearings (BB series):**
- **DVSH, LPN, LPLD** — BB1 axially split single/two-stage pipeline pumps
- **DMX** — BB3 multistage axial split, the Flowserve answer to the Sulzer MSD
- **UZDL** — BB1 axially split double-suction
- **DVSR** — BB2 radially split double-suction
- **WCC, WIK** — BB5 barrel casing multistage, high-pressure boiler feed and injection
- **HDO, HSO** — BB5 barrel casing for refinery and pipeline high-pressure duty

**Overhung (OH series):**
- **HPX** — API 610 OH2, flagship refinery process pump
- **Durco Mark 3** — ANSI B73.1 process pump (the Durco Mark 3 is to chemical service what the Goulds 3196 is to general industry — the default)
- **Innomag** — Sealless magnetic drive

**Vertical:**
- **VPC** — VS6 double-casing can pump (refinery condensate booster)
- **WUC** — Large VS6
- **Pleuger** — SUBM submersible pumps for deep-well, mining, and marine
- **Byron Jackson SUBM** — Oil-filled submersibles, nuclear safety-related service, dating to 1872

**Legacy Flowserve brands (critical for used-market identification — all parts and service go through Flowserve today):**
- **Byron Jackson** — Since 1872; oil-filled submersibles, nuclear, multistage. Common BJ models on the used market: 14H Type S, 14x14x18 DVDS, 3x4x13-1/4 LSJA, 12MH-S, 12x14x18 DVMX.
- **BWIP** (Borg-Warner Industrial Products) — The corporate predecessor that rolled up many brands below
- **Cameron Pumps** — Oilfield centrifugals
- **Dresser Pacific, Dresser Worthington** — Both from the Dresser Industries pump division
- **Duriron / Durco** — ANSI chemical (Mark 3 lineage)
- **IDP** (Ingersoll-Dresser Pumps) — Joint venture that rolled in Ingersoll-Rand pump lines
- **Ingersoll-Rand Pumps** (legacy pump division, not the compressor line)
- **Pacific Pumps** — Heavy BB5 refinery
- **United Centrifugal Pumps** (UCP) — Refinery, pipeline, power
- **Western Land Roller** — Deep-well turbine
- **Worthington** — One of the most historic US pump brands, absorbed via Dresser

When any of these nameplates appears on a used pump, the parts and engineering lineage runs through Flowserve.

### Sulzer

Swiss-headquartered, one of the oldest pump manufacturers in the world (founded 1834), and the dominant supplier of multistage barrel pumps for pipeline and water injection service globally.

**Multistage (the Sulzer specialty):**
- **MSD** — Axially split multistage, BB3. The broadest BB3 hydraulic coverage on the market, with over 10,000 installations in product pipelines, boiler feed, water injection, and nuclear safety-related service.
- **HPT** — Radially split barrel, BB5. Boiler feed specialist for thermal power stations; double-casing design allows piping to remain undisturbed during inner-element removal.
- **GSG** — BB5 barrel, low-density services (hydrocarbons); back-to-back rotor for rotor stability.
- **CP** — BB5 volute-style barrel for high-pressure oil production and refinery high-temperature service.
- **MBN** — Multistage ring-section (horizontal segmental).

**Single-stage:**
- **HZB** — Horizontal, radially split, single-stage, double-suction, centerline-mounted (API BB2). Nuclear safety-related certified.
- **ZE/ZF** — Single-stage process for hydrocarbons and light chemicals.
- **OHH / OHHL** — API 610 OH2 centerline-mount process pump.
- **AHLSTAR** — Single-stage, non-API process (pulp & paper flagship).

**Slurry and pulp:**
- **SJT/SJM** — Heavy-duty slurry (competes with Warman in non-mining applications; not as dominant as Warman in mining)
- **MCE / MCP** — Pulp and paper medium-consistency

**Legacy Sulzer brands:**
- **Bingham** (and **Sulzer Bingham**) — Iconic American multistage pump brand, acquired in 1994 and now integrated into Sulzer. On the used market, Sulzer Bingham MSD pumps are extremely common in 4×6×10.5, 6×8×11, and 10×10×13.5 configurations. Parts and service are handled through Sulzer's service network.
- **ABS Pumps** — Submersibles and mixers for wastewater (Sulzer acquired Cardo/ABS in 2011)
- **Scanpump, Ahlström Pumps** — Earlier Nordic acquisitions

### Grundfos

Danish, privately held, the world's largest pump manufacturer by unit volume — producing more than 17 million pumps per year. Grundfos dominates circulator, booster, and light commercial water pumps and is significant in submersible borehole, dosing, and industrial multistage.

**Submersibles:**
- **SP** — Submersible borehole (stainless steel)

**Circulators/HVAC:**

**Dosing:**
- **DDC, DME, DMH, SMART Digital** — Digital dosing (API 675-compliant options)

**Note on Peerless:** Peerless Pump Company (Indianapolis), a classic US vertical turbine and fire pump brand, was acquired by Grundfos in 2007. Peerless vertical turbine and split-case fire pumps remain in production under the Peerless nameplate with Grundfos backing.

## Positive Displacement Pumps by Subtype

### AODD (Air-Operated Double Diaphragm)

Invented by Wilden in 1955. The go-to transfer pump for anything difficult — abrasive, viscous, shear-sensitive, solids-laden, or where a sealless, no-electricity solution is needed.

- **Wilden** (PSG Dover) — Inventor. Pro-Flo SHIFT Series is the flagship
- **ARO** (Ingersoll Rand) — PD series, EXP series, Pro series
- **Sandpiper** (Warren Rupp, part of IDEX) — S-series, HDF
- **Yamada** — Japanese, popular in semiconductor and chemical
- **Graco Husky** — Broad general-industrial line
- **Versa-Matic** (PSG Dover)
- **All-Flo** (PSG Dover)
- **Verder Air / VA series** — Netherlands
- **Almatec** — High-purity, plastic

### Progressive Cavity (PC)

Rotor-stator positive displacement, excellent for viscous, shear-sensitive, and solids-laden fluids. Mono (René Moineau's original 1930s patent) is the generic reference.

- **NETZSCH** — NEMO series; Tornado rotary lobe; BF biofuel; specialty PC. German, the volume leader
- **Moyno** (NOV) — The US-origin progressive cavity brand (Robbins & Myers heritage); strong in wastewater and solids handling
- **SEEPEX** — German, strong in food/chemical/wastewater; SCT smart conveying technology
- **Allweiler** (Circor) — Oldest PC maker (1860s)
- **Roper** — PC and gear
- **PCM** — French, especially strong in oil field heavy-oil transfer
- **Bornemann** — Now ITT Bornemann; twin-screw but also PC
- **Tarby** — US, solids handling

### Rotary Lobe

Sanitary and sludge/biosolids workhorse. Non-pulsating, low-shear, gentle on shear-sensitive fluids.

- **Alfa Laval / SSP** — SRU, SX sanitary
- **Waukesha Cherry-Burrell** (SPX Flow) — Universal and 200 series (the US sanitary standard for dairy/beverage/pharma)
- **Fristam** — FL/FLR series
- **Börger** — German, strong in sludge, biogas, food byproducts
- **INOXPA** — Spanish sanitary
- **Vogelsang** — German, strong in wastewater and biogas
- **Johnson Pump** (SPX Flow)
- **NETZSCH Tornado** — Non-pulsating rotary lobe

### Gear Pumps

Constant-flow, good for lube, fuel, resin, polymer, and general transfer. Split between external gear (most common) and internal gear (Viking's claim to fame).

- **Viking Pump** (IDEX) — Internal gear, since 1911; the US standard for internal gear
- **Roper** — Gear, also PC
- **Tuthill** — Industrial gear
- **IMO** (Circor) — Historical IMO-AB branding; mostly known now for three-screw (see below), but original IMO gear lines survive
- **Blackmer System One** — Internal gear
- **Kracht** — German, precision gear
- **Maag** (Dover) — Polymer gear pumps for plastics extrusion
- **Witte, Oerlikon Barmag** — Polymer/melt gear
- **Desmi** — Danish marine gear

### Sliding Vane

- **Blackmer** (PSG Dover) — Since 1903 (Grand Rapids, MI). The US standard for LPG, propane, solvent, and fuel unloading; TXV/TX series
- **Corken** — Oklahoma; LPG and industrial gas vane

## Brand Lineage Notes — Acquisitions, Mergers, and Rebrands

The secondary market will show you pumps under dozens of nameplates that no longer exist as independent companies. The physical pumps are still running; the service lineage has been consolidated. This is the single most important section for anyone buying used industrial pumps.
