# Industrial Heat Exchanger Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial heat exchanger OEMs organized by manufacturer, with their major model families. Heat exchangers divide into a handful of fundamental types — shell-and-tube, plate (gasketed, welded, brazed), air-cooled (fin-fan), cooling towers, and specialty (spiral, printed-circuit, hairpin, scraped-surface, block-type) — with OEMs specializing sharply by type. The governing standards are **ASME BPVC Section VIII** for pressure design across all types, **TEMA** (Tubular Exchangers Manufacturers Association) for shell-and-tube construction, **API 660 / ISO 16812** for petroleum shell-and-tube, and **API 661 / ISO 13706** for petroleum air-cooled. Brand lineage (acquisitions, mergers, rebrands) is noted because the same machine often appears in the secondary market under multiple names.

## Tier 1 — Global Market Leaders

The companies that dominate global plate heat exchanger, shell-and-tube, and air-cooled markets.

### Alfa Laval (Sweden)
Co-inventor of the modern gasketed plate heat exchanger. The dominant global brand in plate-based thermal equipment; also strong in shell-and-tube, welded plate, and specialty exchangers. Acquired Tranter (2013) and various process exchanger lines.

**Gasketed Plate Heat Exchangers (GPHE)**
- M series — M3, M6, M10, M15, M20, M30 (legacy "M" series, small to large industrial)
- T series — T2, T5, T6, T8, T10, T20, T25, T35, T45, T50 (current mainstream industrial)
- TS series — TS6, TS20, TS35, TS50 (sanitary/hygienic)
- Clip series — Clip 3, Clip 6, Clip 8, Clip 10 (smaller OEM)
- AlfaFlex — condensers & evaporators for refrigeration
- Industrial Line, Hygienic Line

**Welded Plate & Semi-Welded**
- **Compabloc** — welded plate block (CP15, CP20, CP30, CP40, CP50, CP75, CP120) — a flagship product for aggressive duties, HF alkylation, amine service
- **AlfaDisc** — fully welded plate
- **AlfaRex** — all-welded plate
- **AlfaNova** — 100% stainless steel fusion-bonded (for ammonia, demanding refrigeration)

**Brazed Plate**
- CB series — CB14, CB20, CB30, CB52, CB60, CB76, CB110, CB200, CB300, CB400 (copper-brazed)
- AC series — AC14, AC20, AC30, AC70, AC120, AC250, AC500, AC1000 (nickel-brazed for ammonia/specialty)

**Shell-and-Tube**
- Aalborg — marine and boiler services (Alfa Laval Aalborg heritage)
- Olmi — petrochemical and refinery high-pressure (Italian, acquired)
- Packinox — large welded plate heat exchangers for refinery/petrochemical (acquired 2005)

**Air-Cooled**
- Dry coolers and cooling modules (limited position vs. Chart/Kelvion)

**Tranter Brand (acquired 2013, kept as distinct brand)**
- Superchanger GPHE — GX, GC, GF, GL, GT, GD series
- Supermax shell-and-plate
- Ultramax fully-welded

### Kelvion (Germany — formerly GEA Heat Exchangers)
The heat exchanger division of GEA Group was sold to Triton private equity in 2014 for €1.3bn and rebranded **Kelvion** in November 2015 (name honoring Lord Kelvin). Acquired by Apollo Global Management in 2025. The second of the "Big Two" European plate OEMs alongside Alfa Laval, with equally strong positions in shell-and-tube, finned-tube, and cooling towers.

**Gasketed Plate Heat Exchangers**

**Brazed Plate & Welded**
- GBS / GBH — copper/nickel brazed (legacy GEA Ecobraze)
- K°Bloc — welded plate block (competes with Alfa Laval Compabloc)
- K°Flex — all-welded
- K°Wave — fully welded
- Semi-welded (SW) NT series variants

**Shell-and-Tube**
- Rainey Thermal Solutions (acquired) — petrochemical and refinery shell-and-tube, hairpin, multi-tube
- BEM, BEU, AEP, NEN TEMA types

**Finned-Tube (legacy Searle / Goedhart / Kuba heritage)**
- Searle — unit coolers, condensers, dry coolers (refrigeration)
- Goedhart — industrial/commercial refrigeration evaporators and condensers
- Küba — commercial refrigeration
- Küba Commercial KPB, KCB, KHE

**Cooling Towers (legacy GEA Polacel / Power Cooling Solutions)**

- Dry / wet-dry hybrid

**Machine Cooling (Bloksma / NEMA / Maschinenkühltechnik heritage)**
- Engine cooling modules, radiators, HVAC heat exchangers

### Chart Industries (USA — Ball Ground GA, plus Tulsa and Beasley TX)
Became the dominant US air-cooled heat exchanger group through rapid acquisition (Hudson 2018, Harsco Air-X-Changers 2019, Howden 2023).

**Air-Cooled Heat Exchangers (Fin-Fan)**
- **Hudson Products** — the pioneer of the modern air-cooled heat exchanger; **Fin-Fan®**, **Tuf-Lite®** (axial flow fan), **Hy-Fin®**; Auto-Variable fans; massive LNG and refinery units; world's largest air cooler fabrication facility in Beasley, TX
- **Air-X-Changers** (since 1954) — natural gas compression / midstream; engine-driven, electric-motor, and hydraulic-motor driven configurations; unitized engine & compressor cooling
- **Hammco** — natural gas compression (acquired with Air-X-Changers 2019)
- **Smithco** — induced-draft air coolers
- **Cooler Service Company (CSC)** — gas compression after-cooling
- **Tuf-Lite / Tuf-Lite IV** axial fans — used across all Chart brands, plus sold to other ACHE builders

**Cryogenic Heat Exchangers (Chart core business since founding)**
- Brazed Aluminum Heat Exchangers (BAHX) — the core LNG / air separation / ethylene core block
- Printed Circuit Heat Exchangers (PCHE — via Heatric acquisition... see below)
- Coil-wound (spiral-wound) heat exchangers for LNG

**Through Howden (acquired 2023)**
- Howden Donkin, Howden Turbo Werke, Howden Aeolus — compressors and ventilation
- Recupera heat transfer (some plate/air overlap)

### Heatric (UK — now part of Chart Industries via Howden acquisition 2023)
The inventor and leading manufacturer of **Printed Circuit Heat Exchangers (PCHE)** — diffusion-bonded, etched-plate exchangers for extreme pressure/temperature (LNG, offshore, supercritical CO₂, hydrogen).
- PCHE standard block (up to 600 bar, -196 to +900 °C)
- Hybrid PCHE (hybrid with finned surface)
