# Industrial Centrifuge Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial centrifuge OEMs organized by manufacturer, with their major model families. Brand lineage (acquisitions, mergers, rebrands) is noted because the same machine often appears in the secondary market under multiple names.

---

## Tier 1 — Global Market Leaders

The five companies that account for the largest share of the global industrial centrifuge market: Alfa Laval, GEA, Andritz, FLSmidth, and SPX FLOW.

### Alfa Laval (Sweden)
One of the founding companies of centrifugal separation, originating from De Laval's 1878 cream separator. Alfa Laval acquired Sharples in 2000 and folded it into the Alfa Laval brand.

**Decanter Centrifuges**
- ALDEC series (G2, G3) — municipal & industrial sludge dewatering
- ALDEC G3 — large-capacity wastewater
- NX series (NX 314, NX 418, NX 944) — general-purpose decanters
- Lynx series — drilling mud, oil & gas, construction (2-phase and 3-phase)
- Foodec series — food, dairy, brewing
- SG2 series (SG2-700) — ethanol/biofuel DDGS dewatering
- P3 series — industrial process
- i-Dec / i-DW series — wastewater, potable water
- Prime series — industrial wastewater

**Disc Stack Separators (formerly De Laval / branded as Alfa Laval)**
- MAB / MMB / MMPX — marine and fuel oil purifiers
- AFPX series (213, 313, 413, 513, 517) — clarifiers for chemical, food, vegetable oil
- Brew series — beer/brewery
- Clara — dairy
- LAPX / SRPX — pharmaceutical and biotech

**Sharples Brand (acquired 2000)**
- P-600, P-660, P-1000, P-1500, P-2000, P-3000, P-3400, P-5000, P-5400 — Super-D-Canter decanter series
- DS-706, DSNX, AS-26 — basket centrifuges
- Sharples Super Centrifuge (tubular bowl): T-1, AS-16, AS-26
- M-312, M-512 — vertical bowl tubular centrifuges
- PM series — pharmaceutical peeler

### GEA Group (Germany) — GEA Westfalia Separator
Founded 1893 in Oelde, Germany as Ramesohl & Schmidt; became Westfalia Separator, joined GEA in 1994. One of the two single largest centrifuge OEMs by installed base.

**Disc Stack Separators**
- OSE / OSC / OSD / OSE-PX — clarifiers
- RSE / RSI / RSA — self-cleaning separators (e.g., RSE 200-01-576)
- GSE / GSC / GSD — self-cleaning solid-wall (e.g., GSE 60-06-077, GSE 125-06-777)
- CSA / CSC / CSE — chamber separators
- KDB / KDC — nozzle separators
- HFC / HFB — high-fat separators (dairy)
- Procool — beer clarifier
- ecoclear / ecostream — wastewater

**Decanter Centrifuges (UCD/CC/CF series; "hyvol" and "hysep" subfamilies)**
- UCD 205, UCD 305, UCD 345, UCD 405, UCD 505, UCD 605 — municipal/industrial sludge
- CF series (CF3000, CF5000, CF7000) — general industrial
- CC series (CC 305, CC 458, CC 658) — solid-bowl scroll
- CC 450-01-32 — clarifying/drying decanter
- HyVol B / HyVol B+ — high-volume wastewater
- Hysep MD53C — mobile dewatering skid

**Other**
- Ecorapid disc separator
- procool brewery centrifuge
- viscon & marine separators (SJ, SA, OSD-marine)

### Andritz Separation (Austria)
Heir to a long line of brands acquired through consolidation: Bird Machine, Humboldt-Wedag (KHD), Krauss-Maffei Process Technology, Escher Wyss, 3Sys (filter centrifuges), Frautech, and others. Now arguably the broadest centrifuge portfolio in the world.

**Decanter Centrifuges (Bird/Humboldt heritage)**
- D-series (D2, D3, D4, D5, D6, D7, D8, D9) — original Bird sizing
- D Series Plus
- A-series, AS-series (A4, A5, A6, A7) — chemical/industrial
- CA series — coal
- CB series — biomass / brewery
- HLC, HLF series (Humboldt) — dewatering
- HZ200/HZ400/HZ600 (Humboldt decanter line)
- 3-phase decanter
- Solidbowl Sedicanter

**Krauss-Maffei Peeler & Pusher (HZ / SZ series)**
- HZ peeler centrifuges: HZ 40, HZ 63, HZ 100, HZ 125, HZ 250, HZ 400, HZ 630, HZ 800, HZ 1000, HZ 1250, HZ 1600 (also "Ph" pharma versions e.g., HZ 630/0.6 Ph, HZ 125/2.5)
- SZ pusher centrifuges (single & multi-stage): SZ 21, SZ 28, SZ 40, SZ 51, SZ 51-8, SZ 70, SZ 70-L, SZ 90, SZ 90C, SZ 50-1-B-L
- VZU / TC tube centrifuges
- ZD vertical decanter

**Pusher / Filtering / Other**
- HCT, HCTM thermal & mechanical sludge dryers
- Frautech vertical disc separators
- 3Sys filter centrifuges (peeler & pusher)
- Escher-Wyss screen scroll & pusher centrifuges (P series, P360/2 etc.)

### FLSmidth (Denmark, formerly with U.S. Centrifuge Coal heritage)
Strong in mining, minerals, and coal centrifugation; portfolio largely from acquisition of Decanter Machine, EIMCO, and others.

**Decanter Centrifuges**
- VXP series — coal & minerals
- DorrClone & Dorr-Oliver heritage decanters
- HC, HF series — mining (high-G)
- Fine Coal Centrifuge (FCC)

**Screen Bowl & Coal Centrifuges**
- CMI screen-bowl (acquired)
- Coarse Coal Centrifuge (CCC)
- BHS Filtertechnik (BHS) — pressure / inverting / horizontal vacuum filter centrifuges (BHS-AVH, BHS-HF, BHS-VF, BHS-PF)

### SPX FLOW (USA, Charlotte NC)
Heir to APV, Seital, Waukesha Cherry-Burrell brands; nutrition/health and precision separation focus.

- Seital SE series (SE10, SE25, SE40, SE60, SE85, SE150, SE250, SE400) — disc stack separators (dairy, beverage, pharma, vegetable oil)
- APV separators
- KS series clarifier/separator
- Plenty Mirrlees centrifuges (legacy)

## Tier 2 — Major Specialty Manufacturers

### Flottweg SE (Germany, Vilsbiburg)
A privately held German specialist that competes head-to-head with Alfa Laval and GEA on decanters. Famous for the Tricanter® and Sedicanter®.

**Decanter Centrifuges**
- Z-Series — modular high-performance (Z23, Z3E, Z4E, Z53, Z6E, Z73, Z83, Z92) in 3- or 4-bearing configs and 401, 404, 408, 441, 451, 454 conveyor designs (e.g., Z23-3/401, Z6E-4/441, Z73-4/451)
- C-Series — wastewater dewatering & thickening (C3E, C4E, C7E)
- X-Series — sewage sludge (X4E, X5E, X7E)
- HTS / OSE — sludge dewatering / thickening branded variants

**Specialty 3-Phase**
- Tricanter® — 3-phase liquid/liquid/solid (Z3E, Z4E, Z6E variants /441 designation)
- Sedicanter® — fine soft solids (S3E, S4E, S6E /408 typically)
- Sorticanter® — plastics recycling

**Disc Stack**
- AC series separators
- Belt presses (not centrifuges, but in same product family)

### Hiller GmbH (Germany)
Family-owned decanter specialist.
- DecaPress DP series (DP14, DP25, DP35, DP54, DP62, DP70, DP754, DP55-422)

### Pieralisi (Italy)
Italian specialist, strong in olive oil and food.
- Jumbo, Major, Multinox, Baby decanter series
- FPC, FP-LSP series (food / pharma)
- SPI disc separators

### Centrisys / CNP (USA, Wisconsin)
- CS series decanters (CS10-4, CS14-4, CS18-4, CS21-4, CS21-5)
- THK Thickening decanter
- Volute thickener (CS branded)

### Rousselet Robatel (France)
Pharma/nuclear specialist.
- Vertical pharmaceutical centrifuges: RC50, RC100, RC150
- Annular centrifugal extractors (BXP series): BXP 190, BXP 250, BXP 380, BXP 500, BXP 800
- LX-204 inverting basket
- Pharmaceutical baskets (E series)

### Heinkel Drying & Separation Group (Germany)
Famous as the inventor (1977) of the inverting filter centrifuge. Now part of HEINKEL Group (with Bolz-Summix, Comber, Jongia).
- HF series inverting filter centrifuges: HF 200, HF 300, HF 300.1, HF 600, HF 600.1, HF 800, HF 1000, HF 1300
- HS series peeler centrifuges: HS 300, HS 500, HS 630, HS 800, HS 1000

### Ferrum AG (Switzerland)
Swiss precision basket and peeler centrifuges.
- TA series — pharmaceutical/chemical horizontal peeler
- KMK / SK series — sugar centrifuges (largest share of European sugar centrifuge market)
- ZTS, ZTP series vertical baskets
- Ferrum-Cottey peeler

### BHS-Sonthofen (Germany)
- BHS-AVH inverting filter centrifuge
- BHS-HF horizontal pressure filter centrifuge
- BHS pressure plate filter (rotary)

### Comi-Condor (Italy)
- CF series basket centrifuges (CF-300, CF-400, CF-630, CF-800, CF-1000, CF-1250, CF-1500)
- CMP / SB inverting bag centrifuges

### Tomoe Engineering (Japan)
- Decanter centrifuges P series
- Disc separator C series

### Mitsubishi Kakoki / IHI (Japan)
- IHI HS series separators
- IHI HU horizontal decanters
- Mitsubishi Selfjector marine separators (SJ-30, SJ-50, SJ-100, SJ-200, SJ-700, SJ-1000)

### Siebtechnik Tema (Germany / Netherlands)
- H-series pusher centrifuges (H 350, H 450, H 630, H 700, H 800, H 1000)
- HSG screen-scroll
- KMP horizontal peeler
- Conturbex worm screen centrifuge (CONTURBEX 250 to CONTURBEX 1000)

### Broadbent (UK — Thomas Broadbent & Sons)
Sugar industry heritage going back to 1864.
- Sugar batch centrifugals: BMA, ConBat (continuous batch)
- MK6, MK7, MK8 sugar centrifuges

### Western States Machine Co. (USA, Hamilton OH)
Over 100 years; sugar industry leader, also pharma & cannabinoid extraction.

## Notes on Brand Lineage (Important for Used-Market Listings)

The same physical machine often carries different nameplates depending on vintage:

- **Sharples** = Pennwalt (pre-2000) = Alfa Laval (post-2000); machines all use the "P-####" model numbering
- **Bird** = Bird-Humboldt = Bird-Andritz = Andritz; "D-#" or "A-#" numbering carried through
- **De Laval** (separators) = Alfa-Laval = Alfa Laval; the "MAB/MMB" line retained naming
- **Krauss-Maffei Process Technology** = Andritz Krauss-Maffei; HZ/SZ designations preserved
- **Westfalia Separator** = GEA Westfalia Separator = GEA; OSE/RSE/GSE/UCD designations preserved
- **Humboldt** / **KHD Humboldt-Wedag** = Andritz / FLSmidth (split between them)
- **Escher Wyss** = Sulzer Escher Wyss = Andritz
- **Heinkel** acquired Bolz-Summix and Comber (now all under Heinkel Drying & Separation Group)
- **Tema** = Siebtechnik Tema (post-merger 2017)
