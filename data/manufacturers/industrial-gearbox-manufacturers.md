# Industrial Gearbox Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference of industrial gearbox OEMs organized first by gearbox type, then by manufacturer with major model families. Like mixers, gearbox manufacturers tend to specialize by gear geometry and torque class — a high-precision servo planetary house (Wittenstein, Neugart) doesn't compete with a heavy-industry helical-bevel house (Flender, Lufkin), and a sugar-mill gearbox OEM is a different world from a robot-joint gearhead. Type-first organization mirrors how buyers actually source.

---

## Gearbox Type Quick Reference

| Type | Geometry | Primary Use | Leading Manufacturers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Helical (parallel shaft, inline)** | Parallel shafts, angled teeth | Conveyors, mixers, fans, pumps | SEW-Eurodrive, Nord, Bonfiglioli, Sumitomo, Flender |
| **Helical-Bevel (right-angle)** | 90° intersecting | Conveyors, agitators, packaging | SEW K-series, Nord, Bonfiglioli, Sumitomo BBB, Flender |
| **Worm / Helical-Worm** | 90° non-intersecting | Light/medium right-angle, self-locking | Cone Drive, Boston Gear, Bonfiglioli, Falk Delroyd, Cleveland Gear |
| **Cycloidal** | Epicycloidal disc | Shock loads, indexing, mixers | Sumitomo Cyclo, Nabtesco, Sesame, Onvio |
| **Planetary (industrial)** | Sun + planets + ring | High torque density | Brevini, Bonfiglioli, Comer, Reggiana Riduttori, Bosch Rexroth |
| **Planetary (precision/servo)** | Low backlash | Robotics, machine tools, automation | Wittenstein, Neugart, Apex Dynamics, Stöber, Nidec-Shimpo, GAM |
| **Harmonic / Strain Wave** | Flex spline | Robotic joints, semiconductor | Harmonic Drive, Leaderdrive, CSF |
| **Spiral Bevel / Hypoid** | 90°, intersecting/offset | Heavy right-angle, automotive | Gleason, Cone Drive, KHK, Bevel Gears |
| **Parallel-Shaft Heavy Industrial (custom)** | Multi-stage parallel/bevel | Steel mills, mining, sugar, cement | Flender, Lufkin, Hansen, Falk, David Brown, Renk, Maag |
| **Marine Reduction** | Twin-input, reverse | Ship propulsion | Renk, ZF Marine, Reintjes, Twin Disc, MAN, Allen Gears |
| **Wind Turbine** | Combined planetary + helical | Wind power generation | Winergy (Flender), ZF, Moventas, NGC, Eickhoff |
| **Turbo / High-Speed** | Single-helical, high-speed | Turbine, compressor, pump | Flender Graffenstaden, Lufkin, Voith, Maag, Allen Gears, Renk |
| **Slewing Drives** | Worm/planetary + slew bearing | Cranes, solar trackers, excavators | Bonfiglioli, IMO, Liebherr, Kinematics, Brevini |
| **Track / Wheel Drives** | Compact planetary | Mobile equipment, mining | Bonfiglioli 700T, Brevini SL, Bosch Rexroth GFT, Bondioli & Pavesi |
| **Cooling Tower / Mixer Drives** | Right-angle, vertical | Cooling towers, agitators | Amarillo, Marley, Philadelphia Mixer, Sumitomo Paramax SFC |
| **Screw Conveyor / Shaft-Mount** | Hollow output | Conveyors, screw augers | Dodge TXT, Sumitomo HSM, Falk Quadrive, Nord NORDBLOC |
| **Gearmotor (integrated motor + gearbox)** | Various | General industry | SEW, Nord, Bonfiglioli, Lenze, Sumitomo, Watt Drive |
| **Rolling Mill / Heavy Power** | Pinion stand, twin-output | Steel/aluminum mills | Siemens (Flender Sip), Morgan, Voith, Falk |
