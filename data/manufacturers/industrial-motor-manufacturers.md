# Industrial Electric Motor Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial electric motor OEMs organized by manufacturer, with their major model families. Electric motors are the most universal rotating equipment category — every pump, compressor, gearbox, dryer, mixer, and conveyor depends on one. The market has seen more consolidation over the past 15 years than almost any other industrial equipment category, with five acquisitions completely reshaping the competitive landscape: ABB's 2011 purchase of Baldor (including Reliance and Dodge), Nidec's 2017 purchase of Emerson's Leroy-Somer and Control Techniques, Regal Beloit's 2021 merger with Rexnord PMC to form Regal Rexnord, Siemens' 2024 spin-off and sale of Innomotics to KPS Capital Partners, and WEG's 2024 acquisition of Marathon/Cemp/Rotor industrial motors from Regal Rexnord. Brand lineage is critical on the used market — the same motor may have a Baldor, Reliance, or ABB nameplate depending on vintage.

## Tier 1 — Global Market Leaders

The five companies that dominate industrial electric motors globally: ABB (Baldor-Reliance), Siemens/Innomotics (KPS), WEG, Nidec (Leroy-Somer), and TECO-Westinghouse.

### ABB Motion (Switzerland) — including Baldor-Reliance (USA) and Dodge
One of the two largest industrial motor manufacturers in the world. ABB Motion spans the full range from fractional to 100,000 HP. The 2011 $4.2 B acquisition of Baldor Electric (which itself had acquired Dodge and Reliance Electric from Rockwell Automation in 2007 for $1.8 B) gave ABB the dominant NEMA motor position in North America. Baldor officially became ABB on March 1, 2018; Baldor-Reliance nameplate retained on products. North American HQ: Fort Smith, Arkansas.

### Innomotics / KPS (Germany) — formerly Siemens Motors

## Efficiency Class Quick Reference

International efficiency classes per IEC 60034-30-1:

| Class | Description | Typical Vintage |
|---|---|---|
| **IE1** | Standard Efficiency | Pre-2011 (now banned in most regions for new industrial) |
| **IE2** | High Efficiency | 2011–2016 European norm |
| **IE3** | Premium Efficiency | 2017+ European norm, NEMA Premium equivalent |
| **IE4** | Super Premium Efficiency | Current top induction-motor class |
| **IE5** | Ultra-Premium Efficiency | Synchronous reluctance & permanent magnet (ABB SynRM, WEG W23 Sync+) |
| **IE6** | Hyper-Premium | Emerging (WEG W23 Sync+ ULTRA) |

NEMA equivalents (US market):
- **NEMA Energy Efficient** ≈ IE2
- **NEMA Premium Efficient (EPAct)** ≈ IE3
- **NEMA Super Premium** ≈ IE4

Hazardous area classifications:
- **ATEX** — EU explosion-proof (Zone 1/2 gas, Zone 21/22 dust)
- **Class I Div 1/2** — North America (NEC/CSA) gas
- **Class II Div 1/2** — North America dust
- **IECEx** — global harmonized standard
- **Ex d** — flameproof enclosure
- **Ex e** — increased safety
- **Ex n** — non-sparking
- **Ex p** — purged/pressurized
- **Ex t** — dust protection

Frame standards:
- **NEMA** — North American (36–445T, plus 5000-series large)
- **IEC** — European metric (56, 63, 71, 80, 90, 100, 112, 132, 160, 180, 200, 225, 250, 280, 315, 355, 400, 450, 500, 560, 630, 710 mm shaft height)

Enclosure types:
- **TEFC** — Totally Enclosed Fan Cooled
- **TENV** — Totally Enclosed Non-Ventilated
- **TEAO** — Totally Enclosed Air Over
- **ODP** — Open Drip Proof
- **WPI / WPII** — Weather Protected Types I and II
- **TEWAC** — Totally Enclosed Water-to-Air Cooled
- **TEAAC** — Totally Enclosed Air-to-Air Cooled
- **XP** — Explosion-Proof
- **IP55/IP56/IP65/IP66/IP67** — IEC ingress protection ratings
- **IC411 / IC416 / IC611 / IC81W** — IEC cooling method codes
