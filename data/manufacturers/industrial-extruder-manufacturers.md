# Industrial Extruder Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference of industrial extruder OEMs organized first by extruder type, then by manufacturer with major model families. The extruder world divides sharply by application — a polymer compounding twin-screw house (Coperion, Leistritz) is a different industry from an aluminum extrusion press house (SMS, UBE, Presezzi), which is different again from a rubber/tire extruder house (Troester, NFM, Farrel-Pomini) or a food extruder house (Wenger, Bühler, Clextral). Type-first organization mirrors how buyers actually source.

### Aluminum Extrusion Press (Direct + Indirect + Tube)

#### SMS Group (Düsseldorf, Germany — heir to Schloemann-Siemag)
The global heavyweight in extrusion presses; uses the classic Schloemann lamellar tie-rod / pressure box frame. Now also operates through subsidiaries OMAV and IAS.
- **Direct extrusion presses** — short-stroke front-loader (typically 7"–14" billet, 1,500–10,000 ton)
- **Indirect extrusion presses** — for hard alloys (AA2xxx, 7xxx aerospace)
- **Tube extrusion presses** — front/back-loading with internal piercer
- **HybrEx** — energy-saving press (up to 55% less energy)
- **Schloemann Siemag heritage** (large used-market base, 1960s-90s units of 1,000 to 10,000 ton)

#### UBE Machinery (Yamaguchi, Japan)
Major Japanese aluminum + brass extrusion press OEM.

- Brass/Copper piercing extrusion lines (1,800 ton class typical)
- Tube extrusion
