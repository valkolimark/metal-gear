# Industrial Filter Press Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial filter press and pressure/vacuum filter OEMs organized by manufacturer, with their major model families. The filter press market has seen heavy consolidation over the past 20 years — Andritz Separation alone has absorbed more than a dozen legacy brands, Xylem swallowed Evoqua (and with it JWI/Perrin/IPM) in 2023, and Metso (ex-Metso Outotec) now owns the Larox/Hoesch lineage. Brand lineage matters enormously on the used market, where the same plate-and-frame unit may appear under three or four nameplates depending on vintage.

---

## Tier 1 — Global Market Leaders

The handful of companies that dominate industrial pressure filtration globally: Andritz, Metso (Larox), Diemme/Aqseptence, Xylem (Evoqua/JWI), FLSmidth, and Micronics/Cleanova.

### Andritz Separation (Austria)
By far the broadest filter press portfolio in the world, built through acquisition of 3Sys Technologies, KMPT (Krauss-Maffei Process Technology, 2010), Bird Machine, KHD Humboldt Wedag, Lenser, Netzsch Filtration, Rittershaus & Blecher, Royal GMF Gouda, Delkor, Sprout Bauer, Vandenbroek, Escher Wyss, Frautech, and Guinard Centrifugation. Serves mining, chemical, pharma, and municipal markets.

**Chamber & Membrane Filter Presses (overhead and sidebar)**
- A4 Series — sidebar filter press, up to 2×2 m plates (A4, A4F with membrane)
- SE Series — side-beam, mid-capacity
- OE Series — overhead-beam, high capacity (OE 1500, OE 2000, OE 2500)
- Automat — fully automatic filter press
- MetalPress / PowerPress — heavy-duty mining
- Zero Discharge (ZDE) filter press — tailings, dry-stack
- Rittershaus & Blecher heritage chamber presses
- Lenser filter plates (not a press but the dominant OEM plate supplier, now Andritz)

**Horizontal Plate Pressure Filter / Fast-Opening**
- KMPT (Krauss-Maffei) S series fully-automatic pressure filter
- Larox-style PF competitor models (from Scheibler heritage, via other paths)

**Rotary Pressure & Disc Filters**
- KHD/Humboldt Krauss-Maffei HBF hyperbaric disc filter
- SF Series rotary disc pressure filter
- Krauss-Maffei BF / BFP filter presses (legacy)

**Vacuum & Drum Filters (adjacent)**
- TSF vacuum drum filter (Krauss-Maffei)
- Delkor horizontal belt vacuum filter, linear table filter
- PF / SD / PS vacuum disc filters (Gouda / Delkor heritage)

**Belt Filter Presses**
- SMX, SMS, CPF-H Series belt presses
- Frontec, Frontec S belt thickener/press

**Candle & Nutsche Filters**
- Krauss-Maffei HF / HFT candle filter (Heinkel-style)
- DCF Dynamic Crossflow Filter (Krauss-Maffei)
- Krauss-Maffei agitated Nutsche filter (ANFD)

### Metso (Finland) — Larox / Outotec Filtration
After the Metso + Outotec merger (July 2020) and subsequent rebrand back to Metso (May 2023), the Larox filter line has carried the Outotec Larox / Metso Larox branding. Larox was founded in Lappeenranta, Finland in 1977 and acquired by Outokumpu (→ Outotec) in 2009–2010. Larox absorbed Scheibler and the Hoesch DS double-side press.

**Larox PF — Fast-opening horizontal plate pressure filter (flagship)**
- Larox PF 1.6, PF 3.2, PF 6.4, PF 9.2, PF 12, PF 16, PF 24, PF 32, PF 45, PF 60, PF 78, PF 96, PF 144 (filtration area in m²)
- Larox PF-DS — double-side press (absorbs Hoesch DS technology)

**Larox FFP — Tower (one-stroke) membrane filter press**
- FFP 100 to FFP 1300 (m² range), polypropylene membrane plates

**Other Larox / Outotec heritage**
- Larox CC — ceramic disc filter (pressure)
- Outotec VPA — high-pressure plate filter (tower design)
- Outotec Scheibler — legacy filter press
- Outotec Hoesch DS — legacy double-side press (now rolled into PF-DS)

**Vacuum Filters (Outotec heritage)**
- Outotec disc filter — ceramic disc filter series
- Outotec vacuum belt filter
- Outotec rotary drum filter

### Diemme Filtration / Aqseptence Group (Italy)
Italian specialist founded in the early 1970s, now part of the Aqseptence Group (German multinational, also owns Johnson Screens, Passavant-Geiger, Noggerath). One of the widest filter press ranges in the world. Claims the largest filter press model in the world (GHT5000F Domino, installed at Toquepala copper mine, Peru).

**Chamber & Membrane Filter Presses (overhead beam — GHT family)**
- GHT 1500 F/P, GHT 2000 F/P, GHT 2500 F/P, GHT 3000 F/P, GHT 5000 F/P (plate size)
- GHT-P19, GHT-P20 plate configurations
- GHT 5000F Domino — largest filter press in the world (71 m³ volume, 2,850 m² area)
- GHT 25000P — mining tailings (red mud)

**Side-Beam Presses (ME family)**
- ME 1000, ME 1200, ME 1500, ME 1600, ME 2000 (plate size)
- ME 2000F — iron ore tailings
- Automat 2000 — fully automatic
- Automat 1500 / 1200 / 1000

**Other**
- High-rate thickeners (up to 40 m diameter)
- Batch pressure leaf filter

### Xylem (USA) — Evoqua / JWI / Perrin / IPM Filter Presses
Xylem acquired Evoqua in an all-stock $7.5 B deal announced Jan 2023, closed mid-2023. Evoqua previously consolidated JWI, Perrin, and IPM filter press brands, all of which remain active nameplates on the used market.

**J-Press (JWI heritage) — Recessed Chamber Filter Press**

**MC-Press / MicroCAKE — Large mining**

**Perrin / IPM heritage plates and presses** — still supported as OEM

### FLSmidth (Denmark)
Strong in mining & minerals dewatering, primarily through acquisition of Pneumapress, EIMCO, Ludowici, and the legacy Dorr-Oliver brand. Sold AFT filter bag business to Micronics in 2023.

**Filter Presses**
- AFP IV — automatic fast-opening filter press (tower design, horizontal plates, similar to Larox PF)
- AFP2525 — membrane tower press
- Colossal Mining Filter Press

**Vacuum & Pressure Filters**

### Cleanova / Micronics Engineered Filtration Group (USA)
Micronics (founded 1983, HQ Chattanooga TN; previously Portsmouth NH / Dover NH) was a Vance Street Capital portfolio company until Cleanova acquired it in 2025. Builds both sidebar and overhead-beam presses and is a leading filter cloth/plate OEM. Group also includes SOLAFT, NFM, AFT (acquired from FLSmidth 2023), SFM, CPE, Action, UPC, and Filterfab brands.

**Standard Filter Presses (Micronics / MSP)**

## Standards & Plate Size Quick Reference

Filter press plates are commonly specified by plate size in mm (edge-to-edge). The dominant sizes on the global market:

| Plate Size | Typical Application |
|---|---|
| **150–250 mm** | Lab/pilot (MicroPress etc.) |
| **470 mm** | Small metal finishing, plating |
| **630 mm** | Small-to-medium industrial, plating, metal hydroxide |
| **800 mm** | Mid-capacity industrial, wastewater |
| **1000 mm** | General industrial, municipal sludge |
| **1200 mm** | Municipal sludge, small mining |
| **1500 mm** | Mining, large industrial |
| **1500 × 2000 mm** | Mining tailings, dry-stack |
| **2000 × 2000 mm** | Large mining (copper, iron ore, lithium) |
| **2500–3000 mm** | Super-size mining (Diemme GHT, Andritz OE) |

Plate materials: polypropylene (most common), reinforced PP, CGR (gasketed recessed), non-gasketed recessed, cast iron (legacy, sugar), stainless steel clad, rubber-lined.

Cloth specs are given in CFM (cubic feet per minute permeability), not micron rating.
