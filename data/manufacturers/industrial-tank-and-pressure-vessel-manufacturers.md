# Industrial Tank & Pressure Vessel Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial tank and pressure vessel OEMs organized by manufacturer, with their major product lines. "Tanks and vessels" is an unusually broad category — it spans ASME code-stamped pressure vessels, API 650/620 atmospheric storage tanks, API 2510 LPG spheres, cryogenic vessels, stainless sanitary tanks for food/pharma/dairy, glass-lined reactors, FRP/composite tanks, bolted tanks, underground storage tanks (UST), and rail/truck transport vessels. Buyers rarely search by brand alone — they search by code (ASME U-stamp, API 650, 3-A Sanitary), material (304SS, 316L, Hastelloy, glass-lined, FRP), capacity (gallons/m³/bbl), pressure rating, and jacket type. Consolidation has been less dramatic than motors but the 2023 Chart-Howden deal and the pending 2025-2026 Chart-Baker Hughes merger ($13.6B, $210/share) will significantly reshape the cryogenic and industrial gas vessel market.

## Tier 1 — Global Market Leaders

The companies that dominate industrial tank & pressure vessel manufacturing across multiple segments: McDermott/CB&I, Chart Industries (pending Baker Hughes), Paul Mueller, Enerfab, Pfaudler/GMM Pfaudler, and Matrix Service.

### McDermott International / CB&I Storage Solutions (USA, Houston TX)
The largest engineered storage tank and pressure vessel contractor in the world. McDermott acquired CB&I (Chicago Bridge & Iron) in 2018 for $6B; the CB&I brand continues as "CB&I Storage Solutions" within McDermott. Dominant in large API 650/620 field-erected tanks, LNG tanks, LPG spheres, and cryogenic storage.

- Dome roof tanks with aluminum geodesic domes

- Membrane LNG tanks
- LNG bullet tanks, storage spheres

### Chart Industries (USA, Ball Ground GA) — pending Baker Hughes acquisition (Q2 2026)
Global leader in cryogenic equipment for industrial gas, LNG, hydrogen, clean energy, and industrial processes. In November 2023, Chart acquired Howden ($4.4B), adding air/gas handling (compressors, blowers, fans). In 2025, Chart terminated its proposed $19B merger with Flowserve (paid $266M termination fee) and agreed to be acquired by Baker Hughes for $13.6B ($210/share cash). Shareholders approved October 6, 2025; HSR cleared November 6, 2025; closing expected Q2 2026. Operates 65 manufacturing sites, 50+ service centers globally.

## Capacity & Sizing Quick Reference

Tank buyers almost always search by capacity first:

| Size | Typical Applications |
|---|---|
| **< 100 gallons (<380 L)** | Lab, pilot, portable |
| **100–500 gallons** | Small process, pharma, specialty |
| **500–5,000 gallons** | Mid-size process, brewing micro |
| **5,000–50,000 gallons** | Brewing, dairy silos, industrial process |
| **50,000–500,000 gallons** | Large industrial storage |
| **500,000–5,000,000 gallons** | Terminal storage, water distribution |
| **5,000,000+ gallons** | Large terminals, LNG, water reservoirs |

Volume unit conversions commonly seen on used-market listings:
- 1 gallon = 3.785 L
- 1 barrel (bbl, oil) = 42 gallons = 159 L
- 1 barrel (bbl, beer/UK) = 31 gallons = 117 L
- 1 m³ = 264 gallons = 35.3 ft³
- 1 ft³ = 7.48 gallons

Pressure ratings (common):
- **Atmospheric / Low pressure** (0–15 psig, API 650/620)
- **Low pressure** (15–150 psig, ASME U)
- **Medium pressure** (150–1,500 psig, ASME U)
- **High pressure** (1,500–10,000 psig, ASME U/U2)
- **Ultra-high pressure** (>10,000 psig, ASME U3)
