# Industrial Crusher & Grinding Mill Manufacturers & Models

A comprehensive reference list of industrial size-reduction equipment OEMs — crushers, grinding mills, pulverizers, and ancillary size-reduction machinery — organized by manufacturer, with major model families. Brand lineage (acquisitions, mergers, rebrands) is noted because the same physical machine often appears in the secondary market under multiple names. The comminution industry has undergone intense consolidation over the past 25 years: Metso/Outotec (2020, unified 2023), Sandvik's absorption of Schenck Process Mining (2022), FLSmidth's pivot to pure-play mining and divestment of its 143-year-old Cement business to Pacific Avenue Capital Partners (closed Q4 2025, rebranded as Fuller Technologies), Terex's repeated portfolio reshuffling, and Weir's build-out around KHD-licensed HPGRs all reshape how the same equipment is marketed today vs. five, ten, or thirty years ago.

## Tier 1 — Global Market Leaders

The four companies that dominate hard-rock crushing and grinding worldwide: Metso, Sandvik, FLSmidth, and thyssenkrupp Polysius. These four supply the bulk of the world's large-scale mining comminution circuits and have the deepest aftermarket parts networks.

### Metso (Finland, Espoo)
Formed from the 2020 merger of Metso Minerals and Outotec (creating Metso Outotec), then rebranded to just "Metso" in May 2023 after integration was complete. The largest crushing and grinding OEM globally by installed base. Owns Nordberg, Symons, Barmac, Lokotrack, Nordtrack, McCloskey, Lippmann-Milwaukee, and Shaorui brands. "Outotec" continues as a product brand for legacy minerals processing technologies.

## Size Reduction Ratio & Feed Size Quick Reference

Crushers and mills are typically specified by inlet feed size and outlet/product size. Standard industry ranges for secondary-market comparison:

| Stage | Equipment Type | Typical Feed Size | Typical Product Size | Reduction Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Jaw crusher | Up to 1500 mm (60 in) | 125–300 mm (5–12 in) | 4:1 to 6:1 |
| **Primary** | Gyratory crusher | Up to 2000 mm (80 in) | 150–300 mm (6–12 in) | 4:1 to 7:1 |
| **Primary** | Primary HSI | Up to 1500 mm (60 in) | 100–200 mm (4–8 in) | 10:1 to 20:1 (soft rock) |
| **Primary** | Mineral sizer (low speed) | 1000–1800 mm (40–70 in) | 150–350 mm (6–14 in) | 4:1 to 6:1 |
| **Secondary** | Cone crusher (standard) | 200–450 mm (8–18 in) | 35–100 mm (1.5–4 in) | 3:1 to 5:1 |
| **Secondary** | HSI impact crusher | 200–500 mm (8–20 in) | 20–60 mm (¾–2.5 in) | 10:1 to 20:1 |
| **Tertiary** | Cone crusher (shorthead / fine) | 50–150 mm (2–6 in) | 6–20 mm (¼–¾ in) | 4:1 to 6:1 |
| **Tertiary** | VSI | 50–200 mm (2–8 in) | 0–30 mm (0–1 in) | Variable (shape-dependent) |
| **Tertiary** | HPGR | 50–75 mm (2–3 in) | 5–12 mm (¼–½ in), with fines | 3:1 to 5:1 |
| **Grinding (coarse)** | AG/SAG mill | Up to 250 mm (10 in) | 0.1–1 mm | High (with liberation) |
| **Grinding (coarse)** | Rod mill | 50 mm | 0.5–2 mm | ~15:1 |
| **Grinding (medium)** | Ball mill | 10–25 mm | 10–300 μm | Variable |
| **Grinding (fine)** | Vertical roller mill (cement/raw) | 25–100 mm | 5–20% on 90 μm | Variable |
| **Grinding (ultra-fine)** | Vertimill / stirred mill | 3–6 mm | 20–75 μm | ~10:1 |
| **Grinding (ultra-fine)** | IsaMill / SMD / HIG | 0.1–0.5 mm | 7–40 μm | Variable |
| **Grinding (ultra-fine)** | Jet mill | 0.5–2 mm | 0.5–30 μm | Very high |
| **Grinding (nano)** | Bead mill | <100 μm | <1 μm (down to 50 nm) | Very high |

**Cone Crusher CSS (Closed-Side Setting) Note:** Secondary-market listings typically specify cone crushers by nominal size + head type + CSS range. Standard Symons-style nomenclature: size (3 ft, 4¼ ft, 5½ ft, 7 ft) + head type (Std = standard, Short Head = SH/SHD, Fine = F). HP/MP/GP/CH-CS style uses numeric model number (HP300 = 300 kW motor class).
